# Kirill Kalinin

*From photons to optimisation to tokens to agents.*

Senior Researcher at **Microsoft Research, Cambridge, UK**, working at the intersection of machine learning, optimisation, and computing systems.

### Current research

- **LLM training and inference:** Leading research on multi-token prediction and speculative decoding, including drafter architectures and end-to-end PyTorch experimentation, from data preparation and distributed fine-tuning to inference and evaluation.
- **Self-evolving agentic workflows:** Designing agentic systems that use evolutionary search to improve their context, coordination, and workflows, with applications to kernels acceleration.
- **Hardware-aware computing:** Exploring algorithm-hardware co-design, including compute–memory trade-offs, accelerator dataflow, and unconventional computing platforms.

Some of my ongoing LLM and agentic research is not yet publicly released.

### Public research and code

**[AOCoptimizer.jl](https://github.com/microsoft/AOCoptimizer.jl)** — A public Julia package implementing AOC-inspired algorithms for combinatorial and mixed-variable quadratic optimisation, in which I contributed to the optimisation algorithms design, experiments, and Julia implementation as part of the Analog Optical Computer research programme at Microsoft Research. The associated paper is **[Analog optical computer for AI inference and combinatorial optimization](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09430-z)** (*Nature*, 2025).
